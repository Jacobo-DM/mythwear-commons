MythWear Commons

Open wearable patterns, repair notes, and movement tools for RageDance, DiscoveryMode, Circles in Motion, and everyday life.

MythWear begins where a body, a fabric, and a practice meet. These are not costumes. They are commons patterns: things to sew, adapt, repair, teach with, move in, and pass on.

A small commons rule

Use what helps. Attribute what you receive. Adapt with care. Do not turn living practice into extractive product.

A pattern is not finished when it is published. It is finished enough to be tested, repaired, taught, and returned.
